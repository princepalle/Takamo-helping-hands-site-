Who we are

People helping people, one hand at a time.

Takamo Helping Hands was founded on a simple belief: no one should face hunger, illness or disaster alone. We connect generous supporters with families and communities who need a hand up — and we stay to see that help make a difference.

Our work is rooted in dignity and partnership. We listen first, work with local leaders, and focus on support that lasts beyond a single day.

Our mission

To relieve suffering and restore hope by meeting urgent needs and helping families build a more secure future.

Our vision

Communities where every person has food, safety, health and the chance to learn and thrive.

Our values

Compassion, dignity, honesty and accountability — in every gift we receive and every hand we hold.

What we do

Where your help goes

Food & essentials

Food packages, clean clothing and household basics for families going through hard times.

Education & youth

School supplies, tuition support and mentoring so children can stay in school and dream bigger.

Health & clean water

Medical outreach, hygiene kits and access to safe drinking water for vulnerable communities.

Emergency relief

Fast response when disaster or conflict strikes — shelter supplies, food and care when it matters most.

How we work

From your gift to a family's door

  1. Listen

    We start with the community: what do families actually need right now?

  2. Partner

    We work with local leaders, churches and groups who know the area best.

  3. Deliver

    Supplies and services go directly to the people they're meant for.

  4. Follow up

    We check back, share what your support made possible, and plan what's next.
